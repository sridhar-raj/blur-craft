    What's glass here, and why

    • Sticky navTurns to glass only when content scrolls under it, in pure CSS. Extended backdrop, so colour arrives before content does. .gs-bar--on-scroll
    • On-air cardThick glass, because it sits right over the sun; the rim is aimed at the light. .glass--thick, --glass-rim-angle
    • Station cardsSolid, not glass: they're content. Glass belongs to the controls floating over it.
    • Player dockFrosted glass with Liquid Glass refraction in Chrome (frosted, not clear: it carries text). A real control, so it's the only surface with a pointer sheen. watchLiquidGlass()
    • Fade under the dockProgressive blur: content dissolves instead of being cut.
    • Search paletteThick menu glass over a flat veil; the chrome underneath stops blurring while it's open. .gs-menu, .gs-veil--flat
    • Record dialogThick glass, focus kept inside, the page behind made inert. .gs-dialog
    • ToastRegular glass, announced to screen readers, gone after three seconds. .gs-toast
    • SettingsTinted glass (iOS 26.1's Clear/Tinted switch) and the measured device tier. data-glass, glass-tier.js
