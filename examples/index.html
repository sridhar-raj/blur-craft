Same brief, with and without the blur-craft skill

github.com/sridhar-raj/blur-craft · Open in Chrome to see the Liquid Glass refraction. Brief: a Stillwater meditation hero with a frosted glass player card over a slowly moving background. Both are live; hover and press play.

Without the skill · open full size

  • Glass blur 28px with a flat tint: reads as a grey card, little colour comes through
  • Card covers 15% of the screen, mostly over flat dark sky, so the frosting has little to show
  • Plain 1px white border; no lit rim or refraction
  • Fallbacks for no-blur and reduced transparency (good)
  • 58 fps headless

With the updated skill · open full size

  • The sun is placed under the card on purpose, so the sunset gradient (violet to peach) shows through the glass
  • Five-layer material: 32px blur at 180% saturation, gradient fill, a peach rim aimed at the sun, specular sheen
  • Liquid Glass refraction: the horizon and ripples bend through the card's lower edge (Chrome)
  • Vibrant white text, a waveform progress bar, a 2px blur crossfade on play/pause and the breath cue
  • Reduced-transparency fallback is a lit solid panel, not a broken one; audit 0 fail, 0 warn
